This policy explains how Swad Sangam (“Swad Sangam”, “we”), Bhaktiyana, Badrinath Marg, near NIT Ground, Srinagar, Uttarakhand, India, handles personal data when you use the Swad Sangam Android app.

1. Data we collect

DataWhenPurpose
Name and mobile numberOnly when you place an orderTo prepare your order, identify you at pickup and contact you about it
Account: email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by Supabase), name, mobile number and saved delivery addressesOnly if you create an account on the websiteTo log you in, fill your details at checkout and show your orders on any phone. We do not access your contacts, SMS, call logs, photos or location, and we do not use advertising IDs. You can browse the menu and order without giving any personal data beyond your order. An account is optional.

2. Payments

You pay in cash or by UPI at the restaurant. The app never asks for or stores card numbers, bank details or UPI PINs. If you choose to open your UPI app from Swad Sangam, the payment happens entirely inside your UPI app.

3. Service providers

  • Supabase — database, customer login (email + password) and storage of order and menu data.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting of this website.
  • Google Firebase (Cloud Messaging and Crashlytics) — order notifications and crash reports, if enabled.
  • Google Play — app distribution.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us. We do not sell your personal data.

4. How long we keep data

Your name, phone number and address on an order are automatically removed 365 days after the order is closed. Anonymised order totals are kept for accounting. Anonymous usage events are deleted after about 13 months. Data stored on your phone (cart, saved name and number, list of your orders) stays on your phone until you remove it.

5. Your rights and deleting your data

  • In the app: More → Clear my data on this phone removes everything stored on your device.
  • Account holders can edit their name, number and addresses in Account. To delete your account completely, contact us below.
  • To access, correct or delete order records held by the restaurant, or to withdraw consent, contact us below. We will respond within the period required by law.
  • You may also raise a grievance with the Data Protection Board of India.

6. Security

All communication uses HTTPS. Only authorised restaurant staff can see customer details, and every administrative change is logged. Kitchen staff do not see customer phone numbers.

7. Children

The app is a general restaurant menu and ordering service and is not directed at children.
