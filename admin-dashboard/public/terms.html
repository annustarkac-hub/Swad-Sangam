1. Placing an order

An order is confirmed only when the restaurant accepts it in the app. Until then it shows “Awaiting restaurant confirmation”. If it is not confirmed within a few minutes, please call +91 96757 50485.

2. Prices

Prices shown in the app are in Indian Rupees and are checked again by our system when you place the order. If a price has changed you will be asked to review the new total before ordering. Taxes, packaging and delivery charges (if any) are shown separately before you confirm.

3. Payment

Pay in cash or by UPI at the restaurant (or on delivery, where offered). Online payment is not yet available in the app.

4. Pickup

Please collect your order once it is marked “Ready”. Food not collected within 60 minutes may not be fresh. If you are running late, please call the restaurant on +91 96757 50485.

5. Cancellations and refunds

You can cancel in the app until the restaurant accepts the order. After that, please call the restaurant. If the restaurant cannot fulfil an order it will reject or cancel it with a reason. Refunds for any amount already paid are made in cash or by UPI within 7 days.

6. Food information

Vegetarian / non-vegetarian marks, descriptions and allergen information are provided by the restaurant. If you have an allergy, please tell the staff before ordering. Portions may vary slightly.

6a. Dish photos

Dish photos on this website and app are for representation only. Unless the restaurant has uploaded its own photo, a dish photo is a stock photo of a similar dish (from Wikimedia Commons or Foodiesfeed) and some are AI-generated images. They show the type of dish, not the exact food, plate, garnish or portion served at Swad Sangam. The actual dish may look different. Sources and licences are listed on the Photo credits & image licences page.
